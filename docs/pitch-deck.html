The Solution Slide 3 / 9

QR Point-of-Sale Settlement Layer

StellarPe connects local retail commerce with Stellar's global payment rails — turning any phone into an instant digital-dollar merchant terminal.

1. Dynamic SEP-7 QR
Merchant enters amount in USDC; customers scan with standard mobile wallets (Lobstr, Freighter).
2. Soroban Verification
Atomic smart contract records the transaction on-chain with immutable proof and zero chargebacks.
3. 1-Tap Anchor Settle
Automated SEP-38 quote and SEP-24 off-ramp dispatches local fiat (INR) directly to the merchant's bank account.
